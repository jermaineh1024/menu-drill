1. 1

    Pop the bag forward. Press down with your fingers on the stack of bags a few inches from the open end, which opens the top bag just enough to slide your thumb in. Press your thumb against your pointer and middle fingers and lift the bag. Extend your arm and flick your wrist so the bag pops open. Always pop it forward, where guests can see and hear it, never down or back.

  2. 2

    Twist it into the bag. With the wrapped sub in your dominant hand, give it a little twist as it first goes into the bag, keeping everything over the counter. The top of the sub goes on the top of the bag and the bottom of the sub on the bottom of the bag.

  3. 3

    Crease it, roll it over. Place the bag logo side down on the counter. Pinch the bag along the top and press it into the counter to make a crease. Then roll the sub over the crease toward the guest until the logo side is up. The crease seals the bag around the sub.

  4. 4

    Twist it, mark it. For regulars, minis and kids meals, add a twist after the sub is rolled over. Keep a marker handy: mark the number of the sub on the bag when there are multiple subs on an order, and always mark the name for a phone, online or catering order. Bags can be marked ahead while the subs are being sliced and sprinkled.
