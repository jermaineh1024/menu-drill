1. 1

    Bread on the grill to temper. The pre-cut loaf goes on the grill bottom side down, to warm it, not toast it. Open and close it first so the heat can reach the middle. Keep turning it through the whole cook, and leave it on as long as you can.

  2. 2

    Paper torn, cheese laid out. Tear the paper: 15 inches for a regular. Lay out the cheese: 3 slices. A Big Kahuna gets 4.

  3. 3

    Onions down, as long as the bread. Using the length of the bread on the grill as your guide, spread out 1.25 ounces of onions.

  4. 4

    Peppers on top of the onions. Spread 0.75 ounces of peppers to the same length, on top of the onions. The peppers help cook and marinate the onions.

  5. 5

    Steak on, flipped, lightly salted. Place the steak on the grill with the red-handled tongs, and avoid touching the raw meat. Once it's on, flip it and lightly salt it. A good moment to turn the bread.

  6. 6

    Break 4, bring together, break 4. With the red spatula and the red dough cutter, break the steak 4 times. Bring it together and break it 4 more.

  7. 7

    Onions and peppers on top, break 8 more. Put the peppers and onions on top of the steak and break it 8 more times, the same 4 and 4 as before. That's 16 in all.

  8. 8

    Cheese on top, a few slits. Lay the slices of cheese on top of the steak to melt, and cut a few slits in the cheese so it melts faster. Don't overcook the steak while you wait for it.

  9. 9

    Onto the bread, loose bits scraped on. Use the dough cutter to get all of the steak onto the spatula, and place it onto the bread. Scrape the loose bits off the grill and onto the sub.
