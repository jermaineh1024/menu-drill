Bread 1: panning

The video's line for all three bread lessons: bread is the foundation of every sub. Here is the whole bread day, then the first step.

Panning

  • A clean bread pan and form, the form laid right on the pan.
  • 2 oz of cornmeal, sprinkled evenly in the channels of the form. Too much builds up and blocks the heat from below, so the bread won't bake right. Too little and the bread burns.
  • Frozen loaves into the channels, rib side down. Minis the same way.
  • Slide the pan carefully into the bread rack. When you're done, zip the rack cover closed. It keeps the moisture on the dough while it thaws.
  • Leftover frozen dough: close the bag in the box and get it back in the freezer right away.
  • Roll the rack into the walk-in slowly, so the dough doesn't roll in the channels.
  • Pan after 7 p.m., so the bread thaws properly overnight. Check the amount with the manager or shift leader.
  • Work fast. Frozen dough thaws quickly.

Bread 2: proofing

Proofing wakes up the yeast, so the dough rises fast.

  • Preheat both. Oven to 350°F. Proofer to 100°F at 85 to 95% relative humidity, usually a setting of 4 to 5. The hygrometer at the bottom of the proofer shows the real number.
  • A warmed-up proofer has a mist on the door, not water running down the glass.
  • Rack out of the cooler: straighten the loaves in the forms, and stretch any short ones to the length of the form.
  • First 5 trays into the top 5 rungs, timer for 25 minutes. Then 5 more trays below them, and another 25 minutes.
  • Be quick with the door. Heat and humidity get out every second it's open.
  • Check the dough now and then. It should be slightly tacky. If it looks dry, spray it with room temperature water.
  • 50 to 70 minutes in all. Proofers and the weather vary, which is where the 20 minutes comes from.
  • White and wheat are proofed separately.
  • Done when the loaf just touches the curve of the bread tool: 2 to 2.5 times its original size.

Bread 3: score, bake, cool, stack

A great bake starts with a proper proof. Nothing you do in the oven makes up for a bad one.

  • Score the white loaves, minis too, with the scoring knife: 1/16 inch deep, down the center, stopping 1.5 inches from each end. Wheat is not scored.
  • Into the oven, door closed right away. Bake 14 to 16 minutes. Wheat may take 1 to 2 minutes longer.
  • Keep the rotation going: when the top 5 rungs of the proofer empty, move the bottom 5 trays up and load 5 more below.
  • At the timer, check the color against the bread color chart on the proofer door: golden brown. Take it out the moment it gets there.
  • Cool on the bottom rungs of the bread rack. Heat rises, so the coolest bread is always on the bottom. The cover stays open while it cools; zip it only when every loaf on the rack is cool.
  • Stack only when it's completely cool. Rub the bottoms of 2 loaves together to knock off the extra cornmeal. Pyramid no more than 3 rows high, 18 to 24 loaves a pan, on the front line bread rack, covered.
  • Busy: a smaller pyramid can sit on the front counter by the slicer. Slow: keep the bread covered and sealed, or the air crusts it.
