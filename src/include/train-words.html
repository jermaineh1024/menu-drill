Manager words, in plain English

The words the manager videos use, one line each. Come back here whenever a card uses one you don't know.

Food and counting

COGU
Cost of goods used: what the food and paper you used cost. COGS, cost of goods sold, means the same thing.
Beginning, working and closing inventory
The dollar value of the food and paper in the store at the start of the period. That plus what was delivered. And what's left at the end.
Actual food cost
What food cost was for the period.
Theoretical food cost
What it should have been.
Goal
Theoretical plus 2 points.
Variance
The gap between what was used and what should have been. Red, or negative: too much was used. Black, or positive: too little.
Vendor unit
How a product is packed. 4/1 gal is 4 one-gallon bottles to a case.
Inventory unit
How a product is counted: by the pound, case, box, sleeve, each, bag or gallon.
Reconcile
Match a delivery's paper invoice to what's in CrunchTime, until over/short reads 0.
Purchase journal
The CrunchTime report that lists the period's orders and shows which ones are reconciled.
Post
The last click of an inventory. After it, you pull the report.
Production Pro chart
The daily prep sheet. On hand, plus what was prepped, minus waste, minus what's left, equals what was used.

People and hours

Forecast
A reasonable assumption about upcoming sales, made from the numbers you have.
Coverage chart
For each hour of each day: how many fully trained people the store needs.
Labor percent
What the shifts cost, divided by forecasted sales.
Audible
Changing the play mid-shift when the day calls for it.

Money

P&L
Profit and loss statement. The monthly report on the store's financial health.
Top line
The store's total sales.
Bottom line
What's left after the costs.
Gross sales and net sales
Gross is everything produced, before discounts and coupons. Net is the money that actually came in.
EBITDA
Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. It's the bottom of the store's P&L.
CAM
Common area maintenance. Part of the rent line: landscaping, snow removal, the roof.
Over and short
The cash that should be in the drawer, compared with what was deposited.
KPI
Key performance indicator. A number on the CrunchTime dashboard that shows how the store is doing.
POS
The register system.

Guests

Four walls marketing
Everything inside the store that makes an impression on a guest.
Grassroots
Going out near the store, saying who you are, and inviting people in to try it.
Bounce back coupon
A card handed over at the register as a surprise, to bring the guest back: free chips and a drink, or $1 off.
Shore Points
The rewards points. The simple way to say it: buy 12 subs, get the 13th free.
